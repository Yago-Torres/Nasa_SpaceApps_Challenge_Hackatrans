What a label means

The controlled vocabulary from the scientific policy. Each tag differs in text, shape and colour, so it reads without colour vision.

OBSERVED Observed

Retrieved from NASA or another measured source. Square, solid.

Doesn't prove what damaged the crop.

FARMER_REPORTED Farmer reported

Told to us by the person who farms the field. Leaf-shaped.

Not an independent measurement until verified.

LITERATURE_REVIEWED Reviewed literature

A claim a person checked against the study. Notched like a bookplate.

Search results are Metadata only until then.

HYPOTHESIS Hypothesis

A possible explanation with evidence for and against. Dashed: unproven.

Never shown with a confidence percentage.

MODEL_SCENARIO Model scenario

Computed from named assumptions. Hatched.

Not an observed outcome or a promised saving.

ILLUSTRATIVE Illustrative

Synthetic or teaching example. Survey tape, on purpose.

Never real observations.

MISSING_DATA Missing data

A gap the investigation needs to fill. Empty outline.

Shown, never silently skipped.
