# Rotation comparison with farmer constraints (ABB-17)

Code: `backend/agroblackbox/services/rotations.py`, route module `backend/agroblackbox/rotation_api.py`. Tests: `backend/tests/test_rotations.py` (synthetic fixtures only).

## What it is

`POST /api/v1/rotations/compare` takes a farm profile (the ABB-11 `FarmProfile`) and an optional `horizon_years` (2–10). It returns the **current rotation first**, followed by up to three rule-generated alternatives. If fewer than two alternatives can be generated, the response says why. Nothing is stored.

Every strategy is a `MODEL_SCENARIO`. For the current rotation the sequence itself is `FARMER_REPORTED` (`sequence_origin`), and continuing it over the horizon is a scenario. `verified_impact` is always `null`.

The comparison is **descriptive**. It is built only from what the farmer declared and from crop identity in the catalogue. It does not estimate yield, water use or savings, income, soil carbon or nitrogen, and it gives no score, weight or percentage.

## Inputs used, and inputs deliberately not used

| Input | Used for |
|---|---|
| `crop_history` (main crops, fallow) | Current rotation and its cycle; "new to the farm"; past water regime per crop |
| `local_calendar` (farmer month windows) | All calendar checks, fallow gaps, months with a crop |
| Catalogue family / `is_legume` | Family diversity, same-family succession, legume presence |
| `constraints.excluded_crops`, `market_access` | Screening and constraint checks |
| `constraints.water_regime` | Rainfed screening and water review flags |
| `constraints.available_equipment` / `equipment_limitations` | Whether equipment can be reviewed for crops new to the farm |
| `priorities` (ordinal ranks) | Lists which criteria each strategy changes compared with the current rotation |
| Soil observations, climate | **Not evaluated**: no reviewed crop–soil or climate suitability parameters exist. Each strategy states this as a `NOT_EVALUABLE` check. |

The catalogue has no calendars or agronomic parameters. A crop without a farmer-declared window is never given a default one.

## Rules (rule set `abb17-rotation-rules-0.1.0`)

1. **Current rotation.** Main crops from `crop_history` are ordered by `season_year`. Years with only a `FALLOW` record, or with no record, are fallow slots (missing years are noted). The shortest repeating period of the recorded sequence is the cycle. If nothing repeats, the whole recorded sequence is assumed to repeat. At least two years with a main crop are required; otherwise the status is `INSUFFICIENT_INPUT` and no strategy is listed.
2. **Profile consistency.** The ABB-11 assessment runs first. If it has `ERROR` issues, the status is `BLOCKED_BY_PROFILE_ERRORS` and the errors are listed.
3. **Candidate crops.** Candidates are crops named as main crops in the history, in `local_calendar` or in `market_access`. Screening returns `ELIGIBLE`, `EXCLUDED` or `NOT_EVALUABLE`, with reason codes:
   - `EXCLUDED_BY_FARMER` and `NO_MARKET` → excluded.
   - `IRRIGATED_ONLY_ON_RAINFED_FARM` → excluded. The farm is declared rainfed, and the history only shows the crop under irrigation.
   - `CROP_NOT_IN_CATALOGUE` → not evaluable (family unknown).
   - `CALENDAR_NOT_DECLARED` or `HARVEST_WINDOW_NOT_DECLARED` → not evaluable (the crop cannot be calendar-checked).
4. **Calendar model.** There is one main crop per year. A crop's harvest window starts in its slot year, and a window may cross the year end. Its sowing is the latest occurrence of the declared sowing window at or before the earliest harvest month. For crop A followed by crop B:
   - `INCOMPATIBLE`: B's latest sowing month comes before A's earliest harvest month. The sequence is impossible and is rejected.
   - `TIGHT`: the windows touch or overlap. The sequence works only with an early harvest and a late sowing, and it is flagged for review.
   - `COMPATIBLE`: A's harvest window ends before B's sowing window starts.
   - `NOT_EVALUABLE`: a window is missing, or a year has more than one main crop.
   The fallow gap is the number of whole months between harvest and the next sowing. `min` assumes the latest harvest and the earliest sowing; `max` assumes the opposite.
5. **Alternatives.** Alternatives are generated by enumerating cycles of 2 to `min(4, horizon)` years from eligible crops. A cycle is rejected if any consecutive pair is `INCOMPATIBLE`, including the return from the last crop to the first. It is also rejected if any consecutive pair shares a botanical family, or if it is the current cycle. Each alternative kind picks one sequence with a fixed lexicographic rule, and no sequence is used twice:
   - `MINIMAL_CHANGE`: most current crops kept → fewest crops new to the farm → cycle length closest to the current one → fewest `TIGHT` transitions → fewest unconfirmed markets.
   - `FAMILY_DIVERSIFICATION`: contains a legume if one is eligible → most families → fewest `TIGHT` → fewest unconfirmed markets → fewest new crops.
   - `CALENDAR_COVER`: most months per year with a crop → shortest longest fallow gap → fewest `TIGHT` → fewest unconfirmed markets → fewest new crops.
   Remaining ties are broken by cycle length and then crop codes. The first year of an alternative is chosen so that it follows the last recorded crop as well as the calendar allows (`entry_transition`).
   These rules generate options. They do not judge that one strategy is better. Ordering by farmer priorities is done by ABB-18, which uses a lexicographic rule by rank and no weights (`SCENARIOS_SENSITIVITY.md`).

## Per-strategy output

- `cycle`, `years` (the horizon laid out from `start_season_year`), `transitions` and `entry_transition`.
- `constraint_checks` for `EXCLUDED_CROPS`, `MARKET_ACCESS`, `LOCAL_CALENDAR`, `WATER_REGIME`, `FAMILY_REPETITION`, `EQUIPMENT`, `SOIL` and `CLIMATE`. Each has a status of `SATISFIED`, `VIOLATED`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `NOT_EVALUABLE`, plus a detail sentence. Notes:
  - On a rainfed farm, crops with no rainfed record on the farm are `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, because there is no crop water-demand data.
  - On an irrigated farm, water is `NOT_EVALUABLE` (the allowance is not compared with any demand).
  - `EQUIPMENT` is `SATISFIED` only under the stated assumption that crops already grown on the field are manageable.
- `screening_result`: `CONFLICTS_WITH_DECLARED_CONSTRAINTS` if an excluded crop, a no-market crop or an impossible calendar step is present; `NOT_FULLY_EVALUABLE` if the calendar cannot be checked; otherwise `PASSES_DECLARED_CONSTRAINTS`. Passing means only that no declared constraint is violated.
- `comparison` (descriptive):
  - distinct crops and families, legume presence and legume years per cycle;
  - consecutive same-family pairs;
  - **months per year with a crop in the field** by the declared windows (`min` uses the latest sowing and the earliest harvest). This is potential soil cover from the calendar, not measured cover;
  - the longest fallow gap, crops new to the farm, and market status per crop;
  - `not_evaluable` notes for anything that could not be computed.
- `priorities_addressed`: for each ranked priority, `MORE`, `LESS` or `SAME` compared with the current rotation where a descriptive fact exists:
  - `SOIL_COVER` → calendar months with a crop;
  - `CROP_DIVERSITY` → number of families;
  - all other criteria → `NOT_EVALUABLE`, with the reason.
- `possible_benefits` and `risks`: qualitative sentences derived from the facts above, each saying it is not quantified and needs review. Examples are a legume being introduced, more families, a tight changeover, a long fallow gap, a crop new to the farm, or dropping a crop with a `CONTRACTED` market.
- `conditions_requiring_review`: every `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `NOT_EVALUABLE` check, soil and climate suitability, and agronomist review. For legumes, inoculation and herbicide carry-over are added.
- `evidence_refs`: DOIs taken from `evidence_seed_UNVERIFIED.json` (E-001 legume pre-crops, E-002 rotation diversification). They are labelled `LITERATURE_METADATA` / `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and show the topic match only. No effect size from these papers is reported or applied.

The helper `to_shared_rotation` maps a strategy onto the ABB-02 `Rotation` contract for later persistence.

## Limitations

- Calendar resolution is monthly, and windows come from the farmer, not from a reviewed source.
- One main crop per year. Double cropping, intercrops, cover crops and multi-year stands are not modelled. For example, alfalfa is treated as an annual slot, so its stand duration must be reviewed.
- An `INCOMPATIBLE` pair could become possible with a full fallow year in between. That is a different sequence, and none is generated with fallow years.
- Soil, climate, water demand, pests and diseases, workload, costs and prices are not evaluated.
- The catalogue is pending taxonomic review. Crops outside the catalogue cannot be used in alternatives.
- Generation enumerates every candidate cycle. With all 21 catalogue crops eligible, a request takes about 1 s locally.
